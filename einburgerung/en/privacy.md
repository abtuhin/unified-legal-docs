This privacy policy applies to the "Einbürgerung" app for iOS and Android (the "app"). It explains which personal data is
processed when you use the app, why, and what rights you have.

## 2. Summary

- You **don't need an account**. We never ask for your name or email address.
- Your **learning progress stays on your device**. It is never sent to us.
- We use **no analytics or tracking tools** and no crash reporting.
- To fund the app, it shows **ads via Google AdMob**. Personalised ads are only shown with your consent.

## 3. Data stored only on your device

The app stores locally on your device:

- your learning progress (review levels, answered and wrongly answered questions), your bookmarks and your mock exam results,
- your settings (chosen federal state, translation language, app language, appearance, sound and read-aloud),
- a copy of the question catalogue, so the app also works offline,
- technical ad information, e.g. when an ad-free period ends and when the last ad was shown.

This data never leaves your device and we have no access to it. You can delete your learning progress under
**Settings → Reset progress**. All app data is deleted when you uninstall the app. You choose your federal state
yourself. The app never asks for your location.

**Read aloud:** the read-aloud feature uses your operating system's speech output (iOS or Android). The app itself sends no
data to us for speech output.

## 4. Downloading the question catalogue (server logs)

On first launch and regularly afterwards, the app downloads the current question catalogue from our server. Technically
necessary data is transmitted and briefly stored in server log files:

- IP address
- date and time of the request
- requested address and status code, including the selected translation language
- technical information about the app or device

**Purpose:** delivering the question catalogue, operational security, and detecting and preventing abuse and errors.

**Retention:**
the logs are deleted automatically after 30 days at the latest. We do not combine them with other data or use them to identify you.

**Hosting:** our server is operated by Railway Corporation (USA). Railway processes the data on our behalf under a data
processing agreement. Transfers to the USA are based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR).

## 5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, it shows ads from Google AdMob. For users in the EEA and Switzerland, the provider is Google Ireland
Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google").

**When ads appear:** no earlier than after you have answered 15 questions in total. Ads appear only at natural breaks:
at the end of a question list, below a mock exam result, after a finished practice session, and at a break during longer
practice sessions. They never appear during a question or a mock exam.

**Consent:** before any ad is loaded, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent platform. On iOS, the system also asks whether apps may track you across apps (App Tracking Transparency).

**Data processed:** your device's advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android), IP address, device and app
information (e.g. model, operating system, language) and your interaction with ads (impressions, clicks).

- **With consent**, Google may use this data to show you personalised ads and measure their performance.
- **Without consent**, Google shows non-personalised or limited ads where available. Google then processes only the data
  needed to deliver ads, prevent fraud and handle billing.

**Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). Ads without consent are based on our legitimate interest
in funding the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

**Withdrawal:** you can withdraw or change your consent at any time with effect for the future under
**Settings → Ads → Privacy choices** (shown when consent was required for you). You can also reset or disable the
advertising ID in your device settings.

**Rewarded video:** you can choose to watch a short video ad and then use the app without ads for 24 hours. The same rules
as above apply.

**Transfers to third countries:** Google may transfer data to the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy
Framework (EU Commission adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR). More information:
[How Google uses information from apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. App stores

When you download the app, Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) process data under their own responsibility, e.g.
your account and purchase data. We have no influence on this. The privacy notices of the respective store apply.

## 7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of
processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). You can also object to processing based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR on
grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

Because we have no user accounts and your progress stays on your device, we usually cannot link any data to a specific
person. You can still contact us at any time at tuhin9pro@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member
state where you live, work or where the alleged infringement took place.

## 9. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
